# trade_manager

**What it does:** Runs each open trade by its own strategy's exits (design sections 3 and 4; `manager.py`) and tells the order sender to close it, in full or by half. On each price bar, in order: the scheduled close (20:30 UK every day for the bounce, range trade, slow grind, level reversal and the three session strategies; 20:30 UK on Friday for the EMA pullback and breakout; 15 minutes before big news for the bounce, range trade, level reversal and the three session strategies); the stop at IG (for the Bollinger bounce and the session strategies, where the trail moved it), or the slow grind's 15/10 lock; the range trade's close at the opposite band; the take profit at IG (100 pips on big-news days and in a trend day's direction where the design gives it); the close on a confirmed rejection at a level for the range trade, except for a trade in the day's expected news direction on a big-news day. After each finished 5-minute candle, the Bollinger bounce's trail: once the trade has been 12 pips up, its stop is moved at IG to a tenth of a pip beyond the last 3 finished candles, only ever closer than the stop IG holds (owner, 15 November 2026); a level IG cannot accept is sent as the nearest level it allows, a refused move is tried up to `orders.stop_move_attempts` (3) times, 30 seconds apart, then given up until the next candle with one alert, and IG's buy and sell prices and minimum stop distance are recorded with every move (owner, 17 November 2026); the Asian range breakout, the session sweep and the New York open reversal trail the same way, each with its own `trail_from_pips` and `trail_candles` (owner, 16 November 2026). The stop and take profit are IG's: recorded, never sent. Every decision is recorded in `data/trade_manager/<PAIR>/decisions.jsonl`; each open trade's best price so far, half close and "watching a level" are saved in `open_trades.json` and restored after a restart (`restore`).

`python -m trade_manager.replay_check`: over 26 October to 6 November 2026, through the bot's own code, every trade closes at the same time and price as in `docs/research/all_strategies_together_20261108.md` (161 of 161).

**What it must never do:** Never open new trades. Never widen or remove a stop loss. Never touch a trade the bot did not open.
