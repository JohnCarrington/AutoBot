# One view at a time: all six strategies, 26 October to 9 November 2026

Read-only replay on the stored GBPUSD candles, on IG's prices, through the bot's own code. It runs from Sunday 25 October 21:00 UK to the latest stored candle, Monday 9 November 10:00 UTC (10:00 UK). First with every current rule and limit, then with the "one view at a time" variables: each changed on its own, then in every combination. **Nothing in the bot was changed.** Written 9 November 2026.

## Method

- **The bot's own code.** The ideas come from the strategies' replay (`strategies/replay.py`, all six switched on, as in `.env`). Each idea goes through the safety rules' `decide()` (`safety/rules.py`: each strategy's hours, news and Friday rules, its own limit, most at once 5 not counting the EMA pullback, total risk, funds). Each trade is then run by the trade manager's `plan()` and `step()` (`trade_manager/manager.py`: stops, take profits including the big-news and trend-day 100, the 20:30 and Friday closes, closed before big news, the slow grind's 15/10 lock, the range trade's half close, the close on a rejection at a level, the news-direction exception to it). Bands and rejections come from the analyser (`StateTracker`), as in `trade_manager/replay_check.py`.
- **Checked against the full wired path.** With the current rules, the replay gives exactly the same 167 trades as `trading/engine.py` (strategies, safety rules, order sender in paper mode with a pretend IG, trade manager) run candle by candle over the same three weeks. Every trade has the same entry, close time and pips. The first two weeks are the 161 trades of `all_strategies_together_20261108.md`.
- **The variables** were added on top in a scratchpad script, never in the bot:
  - **Groups:** with the trend = EMA pullback, slow grind, breakout. Reversal = every Bollinger bounce, plus a level reversal that trades against the analyser's trend direction. That direction is the trend's in an emerging trend or a trend; in a possible reversal, it is the trend being reversed. A level reversal in ranging has no trend to go against, so it is not a reversal. The range trade is in neither group.
  - **1. Strong push:** "refused" means a reversal idea is refused while the analyser's state (after the last finished candle) is a trend of the strong-push type in the other direction.
  - **2. Exhaustion:** the analyser moves to consolidation within 60 minutes of the end of a strong push. Acting at the close of the candle it moved on, every open trade (any strategy) in the push's direction is: left alone; closed if in profit; given a stop at break-even (only trades then in profit, since IG cannot take a stop on the wrong side of the price); or closed whether in profit or not.
  - **3. Reversal confirmed:** a reversal trade opens. At its entry price, every open trade the other way (any strategy, other reversal trades included) is: left alone; all closed; or closed only if in profit at that price. Closing trades frees each strategy's one-at-a-time limit, so these versions take more trades.
  - **4. Exceptions:** each UK weekday's direction from (a) the day's expected news direction on a big-news day (`day_news_direction`, as the trade manager uses it), (b) a trend day (`analyser/trend_day.py`), (c) a Monday after a Friday whose daily candle (sell price, open to close) moved 80 pips or more, in the direction that reclaims it. "No reversals" refuses any reversal idea against that day's direction, all day.
- **Measures.** Trades, won, lost, pips, biggest loss, worst losing run (in entry order), pips for each week by entry time, and "both directions held at once": the 5-minute candles during which the bot held at least one buy and one sell. That is shown in hours, as a share of the time it held any trade, with the number of separate times.
- **Three trades were still open at the last candle** (Mon 9 Nov: breakout buy 07:55 at +23.9, level reversal buy 08:25 at +1.4, slow grind buy 09:35 at -0.5, in UK time). They are counted at the last candle's close price.
- **Limits:** two weeks and one morning; 5-minute candles, not ticks; no slippage beyond candle-open gaps; GBPUSD only; £10 a pip for every trade, so pips are shown.

## Reading it

- **Exceptions (no reversals against the day's direction) is the only change that helps much on its own:** +31.7 pips. It also cuts the time spent holding both directions from 31.3 to 26.1 hours. Nine trades that together lost 19.9 pips were left out. Eight were refused as reversals against the day's direction: two level reversal buys on Fri 6 Nov (-14.9, -15.3), the bounces on Thu 5 Nov evening and Fri 6 Nov (two of them winners, +18.7 and +9.0), and the Mon 9 Nov 09:00 bounce sell (-6.0). The ninth, a Fri 6 Nov 13:10 level reversal sell (-4.0), was refused by that strategy's one-at-a-time limit. A refusal at 07:50 had left the level reversal free to take a new sell at 08:55 (+5.8), which was still open at 13:10.
- **Strong push refused** changed only one trade (Thu 5 Nov 07:50 bounce buy, -6.0 avoided), so +6.0. Combined with the exceptions it is the best version: **+1120.9**, 37.7 pips better than now, with the same biggest loss and worst losing run.
- **Exhaustion** happened only twice, and only once with trades open: the two EMA pullback buys of Fri 30 Oct, which went on to their 30-pip take profit. Closing them (in profit or all: the same here) cost 53.3 pips. Moving their stops to break-even cost 60.0, because both came back to their entry first. Too few cases to judge.
- **Reversal confirmed (closing the other way)** costs about 450 pips. Every bounce closed the trend trades against it, including the big Fri 6 Nov EMA pullback and slow grind sells that went on to +66 to +100 pips. It does what was asked of it, cutting the time holding both directions from 31.3 to 8.9 hours ("all closed") or 17.3 hours ("in profit"), but the trend trades were where the money was. Only with the exceptions does it come close to the current total (ranks 2, 3 and 4 of the combinations), because the exceptions remove the Friday bounces that did most of the damage.
- The **biggest loss (-17.4)** is the same in every version: a level reversal with its stop beyond the furthest point. The worst losing run is 7 except where every trade the other way is closed on a reversal (8 to 9).
- Two weeks and one morning, with one strong Friday (6 November) carrying much of the total: these are indications, not proof.
