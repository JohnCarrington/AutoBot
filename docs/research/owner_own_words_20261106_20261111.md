## 2026-11-06 10:19 UTC

The core of the system is the market analyser. This is not a predictive model although it can draw on information from higher timeframes. It is watching and measuring what is actually happening with price and seeing the wider context of what that means. This allows it to determine what type of market we are operating in and therefore what strategy / strategies to enable.

Trending market: a trend strategy that recognises that price / candles are gradually creeping up. This tends to happen before or after a big news announcement. In this slow grind, price doesn't always pullback it just hugs the upper or lower bollinger band, and these types of trends can last all day and move 100 pips.

Trending market: EMA pullback. After a trend has started price can move in a direction and then pullback to the ema cluster. When it does that it can resume in the original trend direction. A trade is opened and follows that trend after the pullback.

Trend exhaustion: Bollinger band bounce. This is when the upper or lower bollinger band ispierced by a candle or its wick end then reverses direction. A trade is opened in the reversal direction.

Ranging market. Trade is opened at one bollinger band and closed when price reaches the other.

Indicators and other data: MACD and RSI provide the bot with a view on exhaustion, EMA slope and fanning provide the view of trend, bollinger band tightness show trend or range, price location relative to pivot and structural levels provide trade management rules.

Improtant timeframes are:

Weekly, daily, 4hour, 1 hour , 5M and tick level
