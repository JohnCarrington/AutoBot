# level_reversal

**What it does:** Design 3.6 (owner, 8 November 2026). Takes the triggers of the strategies' own rejection watcher (the same setups as the Bollinger bounce, design 3.3) only at round numbers ending in 00, S2 and R2 (`level_reversal.level_types`, `level_reversal.round_numbers_every_pips`), and only when price went no more than 5 pips past the level from the piercing candle to the confirmation candle (`level_reversal.allowance_pips`). Entry when price breaks past the confirmation candle. Its stop is 6 pips beyond that furthest point (`level_reversal.stop_beyond_furthest_pips`; the idea's context `stop_price`, read with `strategies.common.stop_price`), take profit 20 pips (`level_reversal.take_profit_pips`). In every state except consolidation, either way, with no direction rule; new trades 06:00 to 20:30 UK, not in big-news windows, closed 15 minutes before big news and at 20:30 UK; one at a time; no close on a rejection at a level. The choices the rules leave open are listed in `strategy.py`.

**What it must never do:** Never send orders itself; its ideas go through the checks in `strategies/common.py` first.
