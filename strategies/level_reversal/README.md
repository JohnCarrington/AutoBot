# level_reversal

**What it does:** Design 3.6 (owner, 8 November 2026). Takes the triggers of the strategies' own rejection watcher (the same setups as the Bollinger bounce, design 3.3) only at round numbers ending in 00, S2, R2 and the daily pivot (`level_reversal.level_types`, `level_reversal.round_numbers_every_pips`), and only when price went no more than its allowance past the level from the piercing candle to the confirmation candle: 12 pips at R2 and S2, 5 pips at round numbers (`level_reversal.allowance_pips_r2`, `allowance_pips_s2`, `allowance_pips_round_number`; owner, 8 November 2026), 12 pips at the daily pivot (`allowance_pips_pivot`; owner, 13 November 2026). Entry when price breaks past the confirmation candle. Its stop is 6 pips beyond that furthest point (`level_reversal.stop_beyond_furthest_pips`; the idea's context `stop_price`, read with `strategies.common.stop_price`), take profit 20 pips (`level_reversal.take_profit_pips`). In every state except consolidation, either way, with no direction rule; new trades 06:00 to 20:30 UK, not in big-news windows, closed 15 minutes before big news and at 20:30 UK; one at a time; no close on a rejection at a level. The choices the rules leave open are listed in `strategy.py`.

**What it must never do:** Never send orders itself; its ideas go through the checks in `strategies/common.py` first.
