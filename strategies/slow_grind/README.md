# slow_grind

**What it does:** Design 3.1 exactly, rules 2 to 4 (rule 1, the two-candle start, was removed by the owner on 7 November 2026; written for a sell, a buy is the mirror image). Once 2 candles in a row (`slow_grind.min_pause_candles`, owner 8 November 2026) have failed to make a new low (below the last low made) while closing below the 21 EMA, it is a pause; the idea comes as soon as price breaks below the lowest point of the pause. A close above the 21 EMA cancels it. No time limit. The choices section 3.1 leaves open are listed in `strategy.py`. Switched on (owner, 8 November 2026): only in a trend, in the trend's direction, with no direction rule; stop 15, take profit 20 (100 on a big-news day), profit lock 15/10, no close on a rejection at a level, may open in big news; no new trade from 20:30 UK to midnight, in the first 15 minutes after the Sunday open or from 21:55 to 22:15 UK; closed at 20:30 UK every day; one at a time (design 3.1, 4, 6; the shared checks in `strategies/common.py`).

**What it must never do:** Never send orders itself; its ideas go through the checks in `strategies/common.py` first.
