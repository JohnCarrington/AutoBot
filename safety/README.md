# safety

**What it does:** Decides whether a new order may go out (`rules.decide`), only from what it is given: the trade idea and its order (size and stop), the bot's open trades, the account balance, IG's dealing rules for the market and the time, and the pause switch. It gives every reason it refuses: the pause switch; an idea the strategy checks blocked; the strategy's `.env` switch; each strategy's own hours, news and Friday rules (design 4 and 6, `strategies.common.may_open`); trades open at once (each strategy's own limit, and at most 5 not counting the EMA pullback's); the size (£10 a pip); the stop at least IG's minimum stop distance, from dealing rules no older than `safety.dealing_rules_max_age_minutes`. **Not built yet:** total risk across all open trades and the available funds (design 6), waiting for the owner's figures. Never contacts IG.

**What it must never do:** Never let a trade through when any check fails or when it is unsure (missing dealing rules refuse). Never be bypassed or switched off by a strategy. Never allow a trade to be held overnight, except the EMA pullback and the breakout, which are held until 20:30 UK on Friday (design 4).
