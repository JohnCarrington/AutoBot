# safety

**What it does:** Decides whether a new order may go out (`rules.decide`), only from what it is given: the trade idea and its order (size and stop), the bot's open trades, the account balance, IG's dealing rules for the market and the time, and the pause switch. It gives every reason it refuses: the pause switch; an idea the strategy checks blocked; the strategy's `.env` switch; each strategy's own hours, news and Friday rules (design 4 and 6, `strategies.common.may_open`); trades open at once (each strategy's own limit, and at most 5 not counting the EMA pullback's); the size (£10 a pip); total risk across all open trades (`safety.max_total_risk_gbp`); the account's available funds at least the margin for one trade (`safety.margin_per_trade_gbp`; read from IG at start-up and whenever a trade opens or closes). IG's minimum stop is not checked before a trade; when IG refuses a trade because of its stop or for lack of funds, `refusals.on_refusal` records IG's minimum (fetched once) and sends a Telegram alert. Never contacts IG itself.

**What it must never do:** Never let a trade through when any check fails or when it is unsure (unknown funds refuse). Never be bypassed or switched off by a strategy. Never allow a trade to be held overnight, except the EMA pullback and the breakout, which are held until 20:30 UK on Friday (design 4).
